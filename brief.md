What makes an authentication method "phishing-resistant"? Which multi-factor authentication methods qualify and which do not?
An authentication method is "phishing-resistant" when it uses public key cryptography and origin binding to eliminate shared secrets and tie the login directly to the exact website domain/Multi-factor authentication (MFA) qualifies when it combines two or more independent categories of verification: something you know (password, PIN), something you have (phone, token), or something you are (biometrics).
